before you ask

Questions officials usually raise in the first demo.

Does the AI decide what happens to a case?

No. Setu suggests possible overlaps and explains why. Officers review the suggestion and decide whether to link cases and which bureau leads.

What if an institution's name is spelled differently?

Matching uses the AICTE permanent ID first, then name variants, address and timing, so small spelling differences don't hide an overlap.

Who can see which cases?

Access follows role and bureau. Officers see their cases, bureau heads see their bureau, and management sees everything.

Is this real data?

Not in this prototype. Every institution, case and number shown is synthetic.
